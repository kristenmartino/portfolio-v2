SpecialtyPulse internal rehearsal — run 02

October 25, 2026. Fixed app release: 7cd9592e01f24cdfe77612ca97dc0852a5753877.

Human participants: 0. Independent reviewers: 0. Planned human study: 0/5 pairs. One AI assistant with prior project knowledge prepared and self-checked all four outputs. The reference comparator was scripted from CSV reports, the answers were known, and the review was not blinded. This is an artifact and process rehearsal. It does not establish human time savings, comprehension, preference, clarification rates, adoption, ROI or sales outcomes.

Tasks and checks

Two candidate tasks were run through the reference-report comparator and the live app: Dermatology / HCPCS 99213, and Orthopedic Surgery / HCPCS 27130. Both compare generated 2022 and synthetic 2023 inputs. The sales scenario date is an invented August 31, 2026. Raw-input Decimal calculations checked the payment/service pairs, and a separate sales-CSV calculation reproduced overdue counts, amounts, shares and ages.

All four briefs contained the required sections and matched the checked numerical values. The two actual browser-saved app files matched the generated export text byte for byte: A was 8,323 bytes; B was 8,135 bytes. These are mechanical checks, not human completeness or interpretation scores. Tool-session timestamps were not used as preparation times.

Finding and fix

Run 01, at release 7b3d18e2298c4b158586a80ac01d28d2811485ed, found that both app briefs lacked a direct pinned sales CSV link. The fixed exports include that link, metric definitions, the selected specialty, open-stage exclusions, overdue date rule and all-specialty pipeline-share denominator. Both linked files were retrieved successfully. The input-file hashes were unchanged from run 01.

The pinned sales CSV reproduces Dermatology's 53 overdue deals / $4,579,800 and Orthopedic Surgery's 47 / $6,272,200. Their shares of the $40,178,200 open-stage total are 11.4% and 15.6%. R1 is resolved for both tested exports at the fixed release. No additional discrepancy was observed within this defined artifact audit.

Human validation remains pending

No intended analyst or independent reviewer has participated. The reference method has not been confirmed as an analyst's actual usual workflow. Similar generated tasks may cause learning effects, and export formatting or links may reveal the preparation method.

The proposed five-pair study's completeness, critical-error, preparation-time and clarification thresholds remain unevaluated. In particular, no 20% human time reduction has been measured. The next step is to confirm intended users' baseline workflow and comparable task difficulty, recruit participants, and collect actual sessions. Passing artifact checks does not establish product usefulness.
