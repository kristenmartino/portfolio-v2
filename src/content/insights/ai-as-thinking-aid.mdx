In [Tarazu](/work/tarazu), AI can suggest reach, impact, confidence and effort inputs. Users can review those suggestions, change the values and record their reasoning before comparing the resulting rankings.

That distinction is useful when a number depends on an assumption. Suppose a team estimates that a feature will reach 1,000 users. The review should make clear where that estimate came from and how the ranking changes if the actual reach is closer to 200. A precise score cannot make an uncertain estimate reliable.

My preference is to keep the calculation visible and treat generated explanations as supporting material. A reviewer should be able to reconstruct a ranking from its inputs without accepting the AI's explanation. Whether this helps a team make better decisions would require observing its use over time.
