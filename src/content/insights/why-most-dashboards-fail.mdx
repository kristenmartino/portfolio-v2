When planning a dashboard, I find it useful to name a question before choosing charts. For example: which specialties have declining average payments, and which procedures account for the difference?

[SpecialtyPulse](/work/specialtypulse-pipeline) supports that review with specialty comparisons and a procedure view. Its synthetic CMS-schema records let someone inspect the calculations and navigation. It does not show that the dashboard improves sales decisions or business outcomes.

Usage measures also depend on the task. A daily operations view might be used every morning; a monthly reconciliation report might be working well at a monthly cadence. Usage frequency needs that context to be meaningful.

I would evaluate a dashboard by asking intended users to complete a relevant review: find the exception, explain the measure and identify what to investigate next. I would also check whether the data reconciles and whether labels are understood. A clear purpose helps choose a view, but data quality and usability still need their own checks.
