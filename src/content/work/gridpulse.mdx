## Overview

GridPulse forecasts hourly U.S. electricity demand using public grid and weather data. It brings reported demand, forecasts, model error, weather alerts, and weather scenarios into one dashboard.

The dashboard shows data sources, update times, model performance and warnings about stale forecasts or provisional readings.

## Product scope

The dashboard answers three questions:

- What demand has been reported, and what does the model forecast next?
- How have the forecasts performed against actual demand and operator forecasts?
- How does forecast demand change under different temperature, wind, and solar assumptions?

## Demand and forecasts

The Overview shows current demand, seven-day statistics, and a chart joining reported demand to the next 24 hours of the ensemble forecast. Its summary leads with the forecast peak and time, followed by current demand and model error. Historical observations are available in an expandable section.

Four role selections change the default tab and the historical observations included in the summary. They share the same demand data, KPIs, and forecasts.

The US Grid view compares balancing authorities using reported demand, interchange, and capacity context. The demand-to-nameplate-capacity ratio is a comparison metric. Available reserves and shortage risk need separate assessment.

## Model evaluation

GridPulse runs XGBoost, Prophet, and SARIMAX, plus a weighted ensemble. The Models view exposes per-model holdout metrics and live error tracking. A separate benchmark compares GridPulse with operator day-ahead forecasts on matched observations.

The interface distinguishes error on held-out historical data from error on live forecasts. Both describe past performance. Published results include balancing authorities where a single model outperforms the ensemble.

The chart identifies whether a band comes from empirical residuals or an indicative range; an indicative range is not a calibrated prediction interval.

## Weather scenarios

The Forecast view lets users adjust temperature, wind and solar assumptions or choose a preset. It shows the resulting demand forecast and explains how the scenario is calculated.

## Delivery and reliability

Scheduled jobs fetch data, score forecasts, and retrain models. The Dash web service reads cached results from Redis, while model artifacts are stored in Google Cloud Storage. A read-only API exposes the same cached data for use outside the dashboard.

The interface discloses stale forecasts, provisional EIA readings, and excluded reporting artifacts. A recent timestamp can still accompany incomplete or provisional inputs.
