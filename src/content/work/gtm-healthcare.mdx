## What I built

GTM Healthcare Intelligence is a collection of seven analytics demos for specialty healthcare sales and marketing. The projects use synthetic and public data to explore metric definitions, practice comparisons, sales funnels, market scoring and natural-language data questions.

## The project set

NorthStar explores a shared metric registry, definition conflicts and reporting use. PracticeFlow and SpecialtyPulse compare practice and specialty measures. ConvertPath examines where deals leave a sales funnel, while SpectrumIQ explores metro-level opportunity scoring. AskGTM and AskPractice provide natural-language interfaces for questions about the demonstration data.

The projects use different views for different review tasks while sharing definitions where appropriate. This is a proposed reporting approach, rather than evidence of adoption by Sales, Marketing or Finance teams.

## Reviewing the demonstrations

A reviewer can compare the funnel and market views, inspect metric definitions and follow the supporting reasoning in generated answers. The [SpecialtyPulse case study](/work/specialtypulse-pipeline) documents its pipeline implementation, sample data and example access policies in more detail.

Mock review sessions and synthetic records demonstrate the workflows. Forecast accuracy, sales lift, customer adoption and return on investment need evaluation in real use. A real deployment would need source-system reconciliation, role-based access checks and evaluation with the intended users.
